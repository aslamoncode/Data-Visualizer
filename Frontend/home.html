Features

Everything you need to start exploring.

Move from a dataset to a useful visual, with profiling and summary statistics available along the way.

Chart recommendations

Get chart suggestions based on the data you load, then choose the view that fits your question.

Interactive visualizations

Switch between bar, line, pie, doughnut, radar, and scatter visualizations.

Data profiling

Review row and column counts, missing values, unique values, and column types.

Numeric statistics

Inspect valid counts, mean, median, minimum, maximum, and standard deviation.
